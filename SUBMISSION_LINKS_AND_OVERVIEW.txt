Project Overview

GradeSense AI is an AI-powered industrial decision support system developed to optimize paper grade transitions in manufacturing environments. The solution leverages Machine Learning, Explainable AI (SHAP), a Digital Twin, Historical Similarity Analysis, and an AI Copilot to assist operators in making informed decisions, minimizing production scrap, reducing transition time, and improving overall operational efficiency.

Key Technologies: React, TypeScript, FastAPI, Python, XGBoost, LSTM, SHAP, SQLite, Tailwind CSS, Plotly.
